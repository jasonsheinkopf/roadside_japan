# Roadside Japan

Hidden Japan is a curated collection of unusual, overlooked, and memorable places, events, and experiences throughout Japan.

Most travel guides focus on the famous attractions: temples, shrines, castles, and the top tourist destinations. This project is for everything else—the strange museums, seasonal events, quirky roadside attractions, local traditions, scenic viewpoints, tiny festivals, abandoned places, unique restaurants, and unexpected discoveries that make Japan endlessly fascinating.

The goal is to answer the question:

> **"If you had more than two weeks in Japan, what should you see that almost nobody knows about?"**

## Objectives

- Build a searchable database of hidden gems across Japan.
- Highlight seasonal experiences that are easy to miss.
- Document interesting places encountered during personal travels.
- Help English-speaking visitors discover a different side of Japan.
- Preserve local stories and unusual attractions before they disappear.

## Current Status

This project is in its early stages and is actively being developed. The data model, website, and content are evolving as new ideas and locations are added.

## Philosophy

Japan rewards curiosity. Some of the best experiences aren't in guidebooks—they're found by taking a random turn, noticing an odd sign, or stopping at a place you never planned to visit.

This project exists to collect those discoveries and make them easier for others to find.
