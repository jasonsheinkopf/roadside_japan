Inside the Powers Asahikawa shopping center, **Asahikawa Ramen Village** gathers eight of the city's most celebrated ramen shops under a single roof — a food theme park built entirely around Asahikawa's signature **soy-sauce ramen**.

## Why It's Interesting

Asahikawa-style ramen is known for its distinctive shoyu broth — a savory blend of seafood and pork bone stock — served over medium-thin curly noodles with tender chāshū pork. Ramen Village lines up eight of the city's best-known shops along one walkway, each running its own recipe for broth, noodles, and toppings, so visitors can compare styles in a single stop. A small exhibit area sketches the history of how the local style developed in Hokkaido's cold climate, and the complex leans into its own gimmick with a photo-ready **Ramen Shrine** — complete with a torii gate — plus rest areas built around giant ramen-bowl and chopstick benches.

## Best Time to Visit

Open year-round, 11:00–20:00.

## Getting There

In Asahikawa's Nagayama district inside the Powers shopping center, about 7–8 minutes on foot from JR Minami-Nagayama Station; large free parking on site, including for tour buses.
