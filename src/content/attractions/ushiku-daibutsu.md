You see it long before you arrive: a serene bronze face rising **120 metres** over the rice fields of Ibaraki, three times the height of New York's Statue of Liberty. Completed in 1993, the **Ushiku Daibutsu** held the record as the world's tallest statue for years and remains one of the tallest bronze figures anywhere.

## Why It's Interesting

Most "great Buddhas" you admire from the outside. This one you go *inside*. An elevator carries you up through four themed floors to a viewing gallery at chest height — roughly 85 metres up — where small windows frame the plains stretching toward Tsukuba. The sheer, slightly absurd scale of it is the whole point.

## Best Time to Visit

Year-round, but spring is special: the grounds fill with flowers, and on clear days the contrast of color against the dark bronze is striking. Summer evenings sometimes feature illumination events.

## Tips

The grounds also include a small petting zoo and gardens, making this an easy, low-effort outing with kids.
