## The world's most famous bánh mì counter

Bánh Mì Phượng at 2B Phan Châu Trinh, on the edge of Hội An's old town, was a beloved local bakery long before Anthony Bourdain stopped by in 2009 and declared its bánh mì "a symphony in a sandwich" on camera. The clip made it arguably the most famous bánh mì shop on earth, and the queue of pilgrims — locals and travelers alike — has been a fixture of the street ever since.

## Why It's Interesting

The sandwich earns the fame: bread baked continuously through the day so it's always shatter-crisp, house pâté, barbecue pork, fresh herbs by the handful, and a secret sauce the family declines to discuss. The full "thập cẩm" (mixed) build is the benchmark order. Lines run 15–20 people deep from late morning, but they move quickly — the assembly line behind the counter is a spectacle in itself.

One honest note: in September 2023 the shop was suspended for three months after a food-poisoning incident affecting hundreds of customers. It reopened, and the crowds returned in force — but it's part of the shop's story and worth knowing.

## Getting There

In Hội An's old town, a short stroll from the Japanese Covered Bridge. Hội An is about 45 minutes by car from Đà Nẵng.
