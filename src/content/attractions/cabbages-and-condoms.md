The slogan says it all: "Our food is guaranteed not to cause pregnancy." Cabbages & Condoms is a leafy garden restaurant serving solid, well-priced Thai food — and every inch of it is decorated with condoms, from chandelier-like installations to a superhero mannequin in the lobby.

## Why It's Interesting

Behind the gimmick is a serious, beloved cause: the restaurant funds the Population and Community Development Association, which pioneered family planning and rural development across Thailand. Instead of an after-dinner mint, you get a condom. It's funny, it's a little surreal, and you leave having done some quiet good.

## Getting There

It's down Sukhumvit Soi 12, a short walk from Asok BTS and Sukhumvit MRT. Book ahead on weekends; the garden tables are the ones to ask for.
