In a long shed beside a Thonburi canal rest some of the most spectacular boats on earth: the royal barges, brought out only for the rarest ceremonial processions down the Chao Phraya. The grandest, Suphannahong, is carved from a single trunk and crowned with a mythical golden swan.

## Why It's Interesting

Up close, the scale and craftsmanship are jaw-dropping — gilded prows shaped into serpents and garudas, lacquered hulls, and the realisation that dozens of oarsmen once propelled these in perfect rhythm. Because it's reached via the back canals, it stays delightfully uncrowded.

## Getting There

The museum is on Khlong Bangkok Noi on the Thonburi side; many visitors fold it into a longtail-boat canal tour, which is half the fun. There's a modest entry fee and a small extra charge for photography.
