On Asahikawa's pedestrian shopping street sits **Gallery PuruPuru**, a small independent art space run by **Hiroshi Abe** — a picture-book illustrator whose earlier career gives the gallery its whole reason for being.

## Why It's Interesting

Before he became one of Japan's best-known children's book authors, Abe spent **25 years as a zookeeper at Asahiyama Zoo**, caring for elephants, lions, owls, and more from 1972 until 1996. That hands-on animal experience runs straight through his illustration work, which has won major awards including the Kodansha Publication Culture Award for picture books and the Shogakukan Award for children's books. In 2011 he founded the NPO Kawauso Club in Asahikawa and opened Gallery PuruPuru as its exhibition space, showing his own work and others' in shows that rotate roughly every two months — a genuinely personal, artist-run gallery rather than a formal museum.

## Best Time to Visit

Open year-round, 11:00–17:00; closed Mondays and Tuesdays. Worth timing to whichever exhibition is currently up.

## Getting There

On 7-jō-dōri along Asahikawa's Heiwa-dōri pedestrian shopping park, about 15 minutes on foot from JR Asahikawa Station; no dedicated lot, but public parking is available nearby.
