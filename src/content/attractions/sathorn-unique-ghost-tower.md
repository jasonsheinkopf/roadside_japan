Designed as an opulent riverside condominium, Sathorn Unique was around 80% complete when Thailand's 1997 economic collapse froze the project forever. Decades on, its grey skeleton still towers over the city — empty balconies, dangling rebar, and an eerie silence amid the traffic below.

## Why It's Interesting

The "Ghost Tower" has become a dark-tourism legend, draped in urban myth and beloved by photographers for its melancholy grandeur and skyline views. It's a stark monument to a boom that burst — a piece of frozen history hiding in plain sight in one of Asia's busiest cities.

## Visiting Safely

The building is **private property and officially off-limits**; entry is both illegal and genuinely dangerous, and access has long been blocked. Treat this as a landmark to appreciate from the surrounding streets and the river — the exterior is the spectacle, and there's no need (or right) to go inside.
