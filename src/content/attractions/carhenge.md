## Stonehenge, but make it Detroit

Jim Reinders studied the real Stonehenge while living in England, then came home to Nebraska and rebuilt it out of cars. Dedicated at the 1987 summer solstice as a memorial to his father, Carhenge arranges 39 vintage automobiles — some buried trunk-down in 5-foot pits, others welded into arches overhead — in a 96-foot circle painted uniform gray to mimic standing stones.

It faithfully copies the original's proportions and solar alignment. Additional car-art sculptures have since gathered around it in the "Car Art Reserve."

## Visiting

- Free, open dawn to dusk, three miles north of Alliance on Highway 87.
- Big-sky isolation; the drive out is part of the experience.
- Best at solstice or sunset, when the alignment does its thing.
