Out on the open Nasu plain, **Nasu Senbonmatsu Farm** is a working dairy turned breezy farm-park — the kind of place where the headline attraction is a really, really good **soft-serve ice cream** made from that morning's milk.

## Why It's Interesting

It's relaxed, free to enter, and crammed with small pleasures: **petting and feeding animals**, pony rides, **archery and fishing ponds**, grass to run on, and farm food everywhere. The signature thrill, weather permitting, is a **tethered hot-air balloon** that lifts you gently above the fields for a wide view across the plain to the mountains. Nothing here is slick or overbuilt — it's just genuine, sunny farm fun.

## Best Time to Visit

**Year-round.** The **balloon** flies only in **calm weather**, usually mornings, so come early if floating up is the goal.

## Getting There

Easiest by **car** on the Nasu plain; taxi from Nishi-Nasuno Station otherwise. Entry is free — you pay only for what you do.
