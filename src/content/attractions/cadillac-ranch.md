## Ten fins in the dirt

In 1974 the art collective Ant Farm — Chip Lord, Hudson Marquez, and Doug Michels — planted ten Cadillacs (models spanning 1949–1963) nose-down in a wheat field west of Amarillo, tilted at the same 60-degree angle as the sides of the Great Pyramid of Giza. The row traces the rise and fall of the tailfin.

Decades of graffiti have long since buried the original paint under layers of spray. Unusually, that's the point: visitors are encouraged to bring a can and add their mark, so the cars look different every single day.

## Visiting

- Free, open 24/7, on the I-40 frontage road west of Amarillo (old Route 66).
- Bring spray paint if you want to leave your mark; pack out your empty cans.
- It's a working field — mud after rain, no facilities.
