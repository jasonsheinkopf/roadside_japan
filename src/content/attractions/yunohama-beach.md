When the August heat settles over Yamagata's inland basins, locals head for the coast — and one of the friendliest stretches is **Yunohama Beach**, a long arc of sand on the **Japan Sea** just west of Tsuruoka.

## Why It's Interesting

It's an easygoing, family-style seaside: **gentle, shallow water** that suits younger swimmers, soft sand for castles, beach huts and shaved ice in season, and a small **torii gate standing out in the waves** that makes for a classic photo. The Japan Sea coast is famous for its **sunsets**, and Yunohama delivers — the sky and water turning gold and pink behind the torii. Best of all, a **hot-spring town sits right behind the beach**, so you can rinse off the salt in an onsen before dinner.

## Best Time to Visit

**Summer** (July–August) for swimming and beach life; the **sunsets** are worth the trip in any season.

## Getting There

Local train or bus from **Tsuruoka Station** to the Yunohama coast — easy to combine with the Kamo Aquarium just down the shore.
