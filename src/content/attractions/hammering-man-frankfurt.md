Outside the **Messeturm** at Frankfurt's trade fair stands a **21-metre** black steel figure with a hammer in a motorised arm: **Hammering Man** by American artist **Jonathan Borofsky**. It was commissioned in **1990** for the tower by developer Jerry Speyer (tower by architect Helmut Jahn).

## Why It's Interesting

Borofsky designed the flat, painted-steel Hammering Man in several scales; the arm moves on a motor so the figure appears to hammer continuously. He says it symbolises the worker in everyone — and Frankfurt's is one of the largest of the series.

## Best Time to Visit

Any time; the piece is outdoors and free. Weekday daytime, when the trade-fair district is busy, has the most atmosphere.

## Getting There

At the **Messeturm**, Friedrich-Ebert-Anlage 49, in Frankfurt's Messe area, close to the Messe/Festhalle transit stops. It makes an easy first stop when arriving from the south on the A5 or A67.
