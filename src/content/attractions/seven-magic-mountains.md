## Neon boulders in the Ivanpah Valley

Swiss artist Ugo Rondinone stacked locally sourced limestone boulders into seven totems, each over 30 feet tall, then painted them in blazing fluorescent colors. Set against the beige Mojave and the distant mountains, they look like something a giant left behind.

Produced by the Nevada Museum of Art and Art Production Fund, the installation opened in May 2016 and is one of the largest land-art works completed in the US in over 40 years. Originally slated for a two-year run, popularity kept it standing.

## Visiting

- Free, open during daylight, about 10 miles south of Las Vegas near Jean Dry Lake and I-15.
- No shade and desert heat — go early, bring water.
- Best light is early morning or golden hour, when the colors really pop.
