If Khlong Toei is Bangkok's raw side, Or Tor Kor is its polished one — a spacious, spotless market renowned for premium produce and ready-to-eat Thai food. It's regularly ranked among the finest fresh markets on the planet.

## Why It's Interesting

The fruit displays alone are a spectacle: flawless mangoes, mangosteens, and a whole pungent kingdom of durian for the brave. Beyond the produce, a superb prepared-food hall lets you graze on curries, grilled meats, and sweets without lifting a wok. It's an easy, comfortable, air-cooled introduction to the depth of Thai food.

## Getting There

Or Tor Kor sits opposite Chatuchak Weekend Market, right by Kamphaeng Phet MRT — combine the two on a weekend. Come hungry and grab a tray at the food hall in the middle.
