Most of Hokkaido's famous flower fields peak in July; Hokuryu's two million sunflowers hit their stride in August, when a farming town of 1,600 people briefly hosts one of the largest sunflower landscapes in Japan.

## Why It's Interesting

Himawari no Sato covers 23 hectares of gentle hillside with roughly two million sunflowers all facing the same way — a scale that turns the landscape abstract, like someone re-textured the hills in yellow. It began in the 1980s as a community project, inspired by a local farm cooperative member's trip past the sunflower fields of former Yugoslavia, and the town has planted it collectively ever since. Entry is free; the festival trimmings (two sunflower mazes, rental bikes, sunflower soft-serve, a "World Sunflower Corner" with two dozen varieties) stay cheap and small-town. As big-name flower spots like Furano wind down, this is the field that's actually at its best in August.

## Best Time to Visit

The 40th Sunflower Festival runs July 20-August 18, 2026, with the bloom usually peaking in the first week of August — mornings give the best light and the flowers face east.

## Getting There

About 1.5-2 hours from Sapporo by car (free parking), or JR to Fukagawa Station and a roughly 35-minute Hokkaido Chuo Bus ride toward Hokuryu Onsen.
