## A museum you play

This is not a look-but-don't-touch museum: every machine is plugged in and hungry for quarters. Owner Tim Arnold and the Las Vegas Pinball Collectors Club have amassed the largest known collection of pinball machines on the planet — more than 700 games — housed in a purpose-built 28,000-square-foot warehouse on Las Vegas Boulevard.

Ranks of machines span the 1950s through the 1990s, from wood-rail antiques to licensed '90s blockbusters. Entry is free; you pay by playing, and the nonprofit donates its profits to charity.

## Visiting

- On Las Vegas Blvd S, a few miles south of the Strip — a refreshingly un-casino way to burn an afternoon.
- Bring a roll of quarters (there are change machines, but still).
- Family-friendly, low-key, and gloriously loud.
