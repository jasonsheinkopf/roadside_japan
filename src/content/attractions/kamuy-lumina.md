Deep in the forest along **Lake Akan**, inside **Akan-Mashu National Park**, a 1.2-kilometer trail turns into something else entirely after dark: **Kamuy Lumina**, a multimedia night walk built by Moment Factory in collaboration with the local Ainu community — the world's first Lumina night walk staged inside a national park.

## Why It's Interesting

Starting at nightfall, visitors carry a handheld **"rhythm staff"** through **eight zones** of forest rigged with lights, projection mapping, and soundscapes that respond to touch and movement — trees glow, water ripples with color, the path itself seems to react. The story woven through the 50-60 minute, roughly hour-long walk is drawn from Ainu legend: the Yukar *"Owl & Jaybird,"* in which an owl-spirit seeks a messenger to save careless humans from themselves. It opened in July 2019 after more than a year of development with the Ainu community of Lake Akan and Japan's Ministry of the Environment, built specifically to minimize impact on the forest's plants and animals.

## Best Time to Visit

**Late May through early November**, when the walk is open; it closes for winter. Showtimes track nightfall, so they shift through the season — check the current schedule before booking.

## Getting There

Buses connect **Kushiro Station** and **Kushiro Airport** to Akanko Onsen. Meet at the Lumina Ticket Booth near the Marimo no Sato pier — the same shore as the marimo boat tour and the Ainu Kotan — at least 15 minutes before your entry time.
