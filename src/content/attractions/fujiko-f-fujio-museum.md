In a leafy corner of **Kawasaki**, just outside Tokyo, is a museum that runs entirely on childhood joy. The **Fujiko·F·Fujio Museum** celebrates the manga artist behind **Doraemon** — the round blue robot cat from the future who is, genuinely, one of the most beloved characters in Japan.

## Why It's Interesting

It's part art museum, part playground. You'll find the artist's **original hand-drawn pages** (delicate, funny, surprisingly moving), recreations of his studio, and then a **rooftop world** where the characters stand as life-size sculptures — including the famous spot where you can pose beside Doraemon and friends. The whole place is designed to make adults grin as hard as the kids.

## Best Time to Visit

**Year-round** — it's indoor and weatherproof, a perfect rainy-day plan. Just remember the **timed tickets sell out**, so book early.

## Getting There

Hop the themed **shuttle bus** from Noborito or Mukogaoka-yuen stations, an easy ride out from Shinjuku. There's no parking, so come by train.
