## A gorge museum with a world record upstairs

The Columbia Gorge Museum's main floor covers what you'd expect from a regional history museum — Lewis and Clark, Native American artifacts of the mid-Columbia, the steamboat and railroad era, natural history of the Gorge. Upstairs in the "Spiritual Quest Gallery" is the surprise: the **Don Brown Rosary Collection**, verified as the largest of its kind in the world.

Don Brown, a founder of the Skamania County Historical Society, spent his life gathering nearly 4,000 rosaries, arranged by size from beads the size of a pinhead to a 16-foot rosary strung from styrofoam balls by schoolchildren in Malden, Massachusetts, for a play. Notable donors include Father Edward J. Flanagan of Boys Town, bandleader Lawrence Welk, 1928 presidential candidate Al Smith, and a young John F. Kennedy, who donated the rosary he carried during his WWII naval service.

## Visiting

- 990 SW Rock Creek Dr, Stevenson, WA — on the Washington side of the Columbia River Gorge, a few minutes off SR-14.
- General admission runs around $10; the rosary gallery is included with entry.
- Pair it with the town's other oddities — Beacon Rock and the Bridge of the Gods are both close by.
