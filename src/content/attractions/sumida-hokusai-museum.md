Toward the Kinshicho end of this walk, a building unlike anything else in the neighborhood appears between low apartment blocks: five interlocking, mirror-bright aluminum volumes designed by Pritzker Prize-winning architect Kazuyo Sejima.

## Why It's Interesting

This isn't a museum honoring Hokusai from a distance — it's built in the actual neighborhood (historic Honjo) where he spent nearly his entire life, moving residences dozens of times but almost never leaving the area. The collection traces his roughly 90-year career (he worked into his late 80s) through original prints, reproductions, and recreated scenes, including work related to his most famous piece, "The Great Wave off Kanagawa." Even skipping the ticketed galleries, the building's exterior — jagged and reflective, deliberately unlike its low-rise surroundings — is worth the detour on its own.

## Best Time to Visit

Any day it's open works; closed Mondays (shifted to Tuesday if Monday is a holiday), with last entry at 5pm.

## Getting There

Roughly 5-10 minutes past Ryogoku Station heading toward Kinshicho — a natural last stop before finishing the walk.
