## The thing under the bridge

Beneath the north end of the Aurora Bridge, an 18-foot troll of steel rebar and concrete hauls itself out of the ground, one hand crushing a real Volkswagen Beetle, a shiny hubcap for an eye. Sculpted by Steve Badanes, Will Martin, Donna Walter, and Ross Whitehead, it took up residence on Halloween 1990 after winning a neighborhood art competition to reclaim a dumping ground under the span.

It has since become Seattle's unofficial neighborhood mascot — climbed on, photographed endlessly, and celebrated each Halloween at "Trolloween."

## Visiting

- Free, always open, at N 36th St and Troll Ave N in the Fremont neighborhood.
- Climb on it — it's built for it — and check the VW for changing surprises.
- Pair with Fremont's other oddities (the rocket, the Lenin statue) for a full quirky-Seattle walk.
