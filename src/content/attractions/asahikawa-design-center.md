Asahikawa has been a furniture-making town since Meiji-era craftsmen settled the region for its forests, and the **Asahikawa Design Center** is where that whole tradition is on show at once: roughly **30 local manufacturers**, about **1,200 pieces** of Asahikawa Furniture and craft, laid out across a 3,300-square-meter hall.

## Why It's Interesting

Renovated in 2017, the center reads less like a furniture showroom and more like a design museum — gallery-style displays, free admission, and an "industrial tourism" mandate to explain how the craft developed rather than just sell it. Asahikawa carries UNESCO's "City of Design" designation partly because of this concentrated furniture industry, and the center is the single best place to see the breadth of it: chairs, cabinets, and woodwork spanning dozens of independent workshops, most of it actually available to buy.

## Best Time to Visit

Open year-round; no particular season matters here.

## Getting There

In Asahikawa's Nagayama district on Route 39, right beside the Otokoyama Sake Brewery Museum — reachable by the same Nagayama-bound bus route from JR Asahikawa Station, or by car with ample on-site parking.
