Rising almost straight out of the **Japan Sea** at Yamagata's northern edge, **Mount Chokai** is so beautifully symmetrical that locals nicknamed it **"Dewa Fuji."** At **2,236 metres**, it's the second-highest peak in Tohoku and one of its most striking sights from miles around.

## Why It's Interesting

It's a mountain for every level. Serious hikers tackle the long trails to the crater rim through **alpine flower meadows** that bloom in July and August, often crossing **snowfields that survive well into summer** — a surreal thrill in the heat. But you don't have to summit to enjoy it: at the base, **Motodaki Fukuryusui** is an enchanting spot where **icy spring water bursts straight out of mossy volcanic rock** into a series of falls, staying cold and refreshing through the hottest August day. Cool air, snow patches, and clear water make Chokai a perfect summer antidote to the lowland heat.

## Best Time to Visit

**Summer to autumn** (roughly July–October) for hiking and the meadows; the lower Motodaki falls are a cool, easy visit anytime in season.

## Getting There

Easiest by **car** to the trailheads or Motodaki; bus and taxi connect from **Yuza** and Sakata.
