Tucked into the hills of southern Chiba, **Oyama Senmaida** — "the thousand rice fields of Oyama" — is the nearest classic **rice-terrace** landscape to Tokyo, and one of the prettiest. Some 375 tiny paddies step down the slope in a patchwork of curved banks.

## Why It's Interesting

The terraces change completely with the season. In **spring**, freshly flooded, each little paddy becomes a mirror, and the whole hillside reflects the sky and clouds. In **summer** it's vivid green; in **autumn**, gold. Best of all, on cold-season evenings volunteers stage a **candle-light illumination**, dotting the terraces with thousands of LED "candles" that trace the curving banks in the dark. It's farmland as quiet theater, kept alive by locals.

## Best Time to Visit

**Spring** for sky reflections, and the **cold-season evenings** for the LED illumination — check the schedule before going.

## Getting There

Easiest by **car** from Kamogawa; bus or taxi from Awa-Kamogawa Station otherwise. Combine it with the southern Boso coast.
