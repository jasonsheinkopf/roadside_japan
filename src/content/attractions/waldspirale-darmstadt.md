Drive south of Frankfurt for a moment and you find **Darmstadt's Waldspirale** ("forest spiral"), a U-shaped residential complex of **105 apartments** designed by the Austrian artist **Friedensreich Hundertwasser** and completed in **2000**. It is the last building he saw finished; he died aboard the Queen Mary 2 in 2000 while travelling to see it.

## Why It's Interesting

Hundertwasser rejected straight lines, and it shows: the façade doesn't follow a grid, there are more than **1,000 windows**, no two the same, and "tree tenants" grow from windows and balconies. The roof is a planted green roof, and the inner courtyard has a playground and a small artificial lake.

## Best Time to Visit

Any time. Spring and summer, when the planting is in leaf, look best. The rooftop restaurants and bars that once let the public up no longer operate, so plan on admiring it from outside.

## Getting There

At **Bad Nauheimer Straße 2, 64289 Darmstadt**. It is roughly a 40-minute detour from the A5 on the way between Heilbronn and Frankfurt — a good stretch-your-legs stop rather than a destination for hours.
