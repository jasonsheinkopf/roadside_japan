Japan takes its **summer fireworks** seriously, and few shows are more revered than the **Akagawa Fireworks Festival** in Tsuruoka, which openly aims to be **"Japan's most moving fireworks display."**

## Why It's Interesting

On one mid-August night, the nation's top pyrotechnicians launch around **12,000 fireworks** along a vast **700-metre** stretch of the Akagawa riverbank, building to a grand finale **choreographed to music**. It's less a backyard fireworks night than a full orchestral performance in the sky — waves of color and light timed to a soundtrack, reflected in the river. It regularly tops fans' rankings of Japanese **hanabi**, and for a family in Yamagata in August, it's an unforgettable evening.

## Best Time to Visit

One evening in **mid-August** — the **2026 show is scheduled for Saturday, August 15**. Confirm the date and, ideally, book a riverside seat in advance.

## Getting There

Take the **shuttle bus from Tsuruoka Station** to the riverbank; roads jam badly, so skip the car and arrive early for the best spot.
