Yokohama's Minato Mirai waterfront hosts frequent small fireworks shows throughout the year, but August 24, 2026 brings something bigger: a full festival built around promoting the city's upcoming GREEN×EXPO 2027.

## Why It's Interesting

Rather than a standard hanabi night, this is billed as a full festival — live music and street performers along the harbor promenade building toward a large-scale fireworks finale over Yokohama Bay. It's tied specifically to raising awareness for GREEN×EXPO 2027, the international horticultural exposition Yokohama will host the following year, which makes this a one-time-feeling event rather than a fixture you can count on repeating annually in the same form.

## Best Time to Visit

The single date is Saturday, August 24, 2026, with the festival building through the evening to a fireworks finale.

## Getting There

Five minutes on foot from Minato Mirai Station to Rinko Park, or about twelve minutes from Sakuragicho Station; expect heavy crowds along the whole waterfront that evening.
